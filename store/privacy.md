# Privacy policy (draft)

Block Puzzle stores only a local high score on the device using shared preferences. That score never leaves the device.

The game shows ads from Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and rewarded). Google may collect advertising identifiers and approximate location as described in Google's advertising privacy policy. On iOS, the app may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission to deliver personalized ads. You can refuse; non-personalized ads still work.

The app does not create an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not sell personal information.
